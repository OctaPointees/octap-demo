1. Product Background
   Consumer credit and loyalty reward systems currently face critical flaws, such as vulnerability to internal fraud, high integration costs, opaque accounting disputes, and poor user experience. Traditional centralized databases are frequent targets for unauthorized point minting and manipulation. OctaPoint (OctaP) solves this by offering an enterprise-grade Credit-as-a-Service (CaaS) platform built on the Sui blockchain. It provides enterprise security, auditability, and a seamless Web2 experience. By delivering turnkey TypeScript developer SDKs and an AI-ready Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, OctaP enables businesses to quickly deploy highly secure, isolated, and custom loyalty infrastructures without managing complex blockchain operations.
2. Existing Systems
   2.1 UrBox
   Description: A leading digital rewards and corporate gifting platform in Vietnam.
   Pros: Extensive partnership network and seamless API integration for enterprise clients.
   Cons: Built on a centralized architecture, meaning partners must fully trust UrBox's internal ledger. It operates primarily as a B2C voucher marketplace rather than a white-label backend infrastructure for independent merchants to run isolated loyalty rules.
   2.2 Talon.one
   Description: Global API-first promotion and loyalty engines.
   Pros: Highly flexible rule engines and robust developer documentation.
   Cons: As pure Web2 SaaS platforms, they remain vulnerable to internal database manipulation. They have high enterprise subscription costs and lack an immutable, cryptographically verifiable transaction history for flawless auditing.
3. Business Opportunity
   The current loyalty market is fragmented between traditional voucher marketplaces (which lack independent brand control) and pure SaaS infrastructure providers (which require high trust and are vulnerable to internal fraud). While Web3 blockchain solutions offer perfect security and transparency, they suffer from massive adoption barriers due to crypto wallets and gas fees.
   OctaP bridges this gap by offering a Web3-backed, Web2-friendly loyalty infrastructure. By leveraging Enoki's zkLogin and Sponsored Transactions on the Sui blockchain, OctaP allows merchants to launch fraud-proof, isolated loyalty programs with zero blockchain friction for their end-users. This presents a massive opportunity to provide a highly secure, object-centric CaaS platform to mid-sized enterprises at a fraction of traditional development costs.
4. Software Product Vision
   OctaP is an end-to-end infrastructure platform designed to modernize enterprise loyalty ecosystems. Built on the Sui blockchain, OctaP bridges high-performance decentralized ledger technology with accessible Web2 user experiences.
   For Merchants & Business Partners: OctaP empowers businesses with a modular, low-overhead platform to launch customized, strictly isolated loyalty programs. The Real-Time Merchant Dashboard provides analytics, campaign management, and full auditability of points.
   For Developers & System Integrators: OctaP abstracts blockchain complexity by providing robust infrastructure tools—including high-level TypeScript SDKs for rapid integration with custom apps or POS systems. AI-Ready MCP Server endpoints enable AI agents to securely query balances and execute rules.
   For End-Users: OctaP delivers a rewards experience that integrates effortlessly into daily activities. Through a unified interface and quick phone-number registration (abstracting Web3 wallets), users can earn points at checkout and redeem them for merchant-specific rewards.
5. Project Scope & Limitations
   5.1 Major Features
   FE-01: Modular Merchant Dashboard: Allow business owners to configure campaigns, budgets, and track ROI via real-time analytics.
   FE-02: Secure Point Issuance (Earn): Automate the issuance of tenant-isolated points via POS integration and SDKs.
   FE-03: Cryptographic Point Redemption: Enable users to securely redeem accumulated points for digital vouchers using zkLogin consent.
   FE-04: AI-Ready MCP Server: Expose endpoints for AI agents and chatbots to query balances and manage campaigns within RBAC bounds.
   FE-05: Developer SDK Suite: Provide turnkey TypeScript libraries for frictionless POS and Web application integration.
   5.2 Limitations & Exclusions
   LI-1: Strict Tenant Isolation: Points are strictly isolated per merchant. There is no cross-tenant exchange rate or Peer-to-Peer (P2P) point transfer
   LI-2: No Fiat Cash-Out: OctaPoints (OCP) are promotional reward credits. They cannot be directly converted, withdrawn, or cashed out into fiat currency.
   LI-3: Test Harness Interfaces: The provided end-user applications are developed strictly as integration references to demonstrate SDK capabilities, not as fully commercialized consumer marketplace apps
