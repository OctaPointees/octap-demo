The visual language of OctaP, written down.

OctaP is a Credit-as-a-Service loyalty platform on Sui — an operator console, a merchant dashboard and an end-user wallet built as one design draft. This page is the extracted specification: every token, component state and interaction pattern the prototype actually uses, taken from the source rather than redrawn.

§1

Color

One daisyUI theme, declared in src/theme.css. Ten semantic roles plus a three-step neutral ramp. Hierarchy in the product is carried by opacity, not by extra text colors — there is exactly one ink. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

§6

Charts

No chart library — four hand-rolled SVG primitives in components/charts/, all sharing one geometry: viewBox 0 0 800 H, padding {t:12 r:12 b:24 l:48}, axis maxima rounded by niceMax(), grid lines at 8% of the ink, labels at 50%.

AreaChart · h 240 · 2px line + 0.22→0 fill

BarChart · h 220 · grouped, band 65%, rx 2

Donut · r 40 · stroke 14 · base-200 track

  • Bronze 42.0%
  • Silver 28.0%
  • Gold 19.0%
  • Platinum 11.0%

Sparkline · 100×30 · non-scaling 1.5px stroke

Embedded in every StatCard at h-8 w-24. Scale is local to the series (min→max), so it reads shape, never magnitude.

interaction

Area and bar charts track the pointer: the area chart snaps to the nearest index and draws a dashed crosshair at 25% opacity with r=4 dots; the bar chart dims non-hovered bands to 50%. Both float a bordered tooltip that flips to the other side past the midpoint so it never clips.

Interaction rules

state

Never optimistic

Mutations disable their trigger and show an inline spinner with a narrative label; the result arrives as a toast, and every query is invalidated so dashboards, ledger and audit move together.

rbac

Permissions are explained, not just hidden

Unavailable actions disappear and an info alert says why — "Only the workspace owner can create keys or change MCP permissions."

risk

Destructive actions cost a sentence

Suspend, reject, revoke and end-campaign all route through a confirm dialog; suspend and reject require a typed reason of at least 10 characters, and the copy says it is recorded on-chain.

search

One search field, two sources

250ms debounce, local page-name matches merged above async results, full ↑/↓/Enter/Esc keyboard handling, spinner inside the input.

§9

Findings

Gaps between the system as specified and the system as shipped, worst first.

critical

Neither typeface loads

--font-sans: "Google Sans" and --font-mono: "Space Mono" are declared with no @font-face, link or import anywhere. The app renders in the system UI font. Space Mono is on Google Fonts; Google Sans is not distributed there and needs a licensed self-host or a substitute.

warning

The theme is called "dark" and is light

theme.css sets name: "dark" with color-scheme: dark, but base-100 is white and base-content is black. color-scheme: dark also makes the browser paint native controls — scrollbars, date pickers, the Settings color input — in dark styling on white surfaces.

warning

Favicon is off-brand

public/favicon.svg is #863bff purple, unrelated to the #9f0261 primary used by the in-app logo tile.

warning

12px carries the interface

text-xs appears 126 times against 87 for text-sm, and most secondary text also sits at 60% opacity — roughly 4.5:1 contrast on white. Worth a pass for readability.

info

info and accent are the same hex

Both are #33bbf6, so the two semantic roles cannot be told apart. Shift one, or collapse them into a single documented role.

info

Tier colors bypass the token system

The four tier hexes are hardcoded twice — as badge classes in Display.tsx and as donut fills in the merchant dashboard. They are the only hardcoded colors outside the seed data.
